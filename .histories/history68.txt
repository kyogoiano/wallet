### What is now properly covered

* **Traceability:** requirements/invariants/triads map to concrete task IDs and verification tests.
* **Native algebra:** `DecisionValue`, typed questions, and sealed outcomes are implemented before evaluator infrastructure.
* **Generic safety:** `EvaluatedQuestion<T>` preserves `DecisionQuestion<T> → DecisionOutcome<T>`, including runtime witness validation.
* **Grounding:** evidence gets canonical hashing rather than merely carrying textual provenance.
* **Domain catalog:** questions are first-class and typed.
* **Anti-coercion:** unavailable results cannot become `false`, `0`, or synthetic risk.
* **Evaluator isolation:** timeout/failure handling is explicitly separated from the transactional path.
* **Benchmark gates:** contract, grounding, correctness, calibration, and operational/deterministic behavior are executable tasks rather than merely architectural prose.
* **Airgap:** the final task verifies both package dependency direction and the hot-path latency boundary.

There is also an important improvement over the previous version: the unavailable test now explicitly checks **structural absence of score/confidence**, rather than relying on an `Optional.empty()` accessor.  That's the right interpretation of the architecture.
